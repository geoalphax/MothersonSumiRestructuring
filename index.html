5

Five ideas that unlock every restructuring

1

A demerger copies ownership

Whoever owns the parent on the record date owns the new company in the same proportion. So the order of steps decides who ends up owning what.

2

A swap ratio is a price paid in shares

Target value per share ÷ acquirer value per share. Too high moves value to the target's owners; too low moves it the other way.

3

Cancel what you'd own of yourself

When a holdco merges in, its stake in the acquirer is cancelled. When a parent absorbs a subsidiary, it issues nothing for its own stake.

4

Look through to the businesses

Judge a deal by each person's slice of each business, not by % of one company. Multiply along the chain, add the chains.

5

Value per share is the fairness test

(Acquirer value + target value − cancelled shares' value) ÷ new share count. Unchanged means nobody was short-changed.
